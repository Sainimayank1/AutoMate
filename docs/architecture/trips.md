# Trips (M7)

Keeps each vehicle's journeys: where, when, how far, what kind of trip, and (in later phases) what it cost, the route and the plan. Product scope: [MODULES.md M7](../MODULES.md#module-7--trips--journey-tracking); money rules: [expenses.md](expenses.md); look: the Stitch "Trips Overview", "Log Trip Form", "Trip Details" and "Trip Types Dialog" screens through the `automate-design` skill.

**Ownership:** Trips owns journeys, their distance and trip types. The vehicle's odometer is Garage's; trips add readings to its **one odometer history** (below). Mileage stays Fuel's: Trips never calculates efficiency. Money (phase 3) goes through `LinkedExpensesService` with source `TRIP`.

## 1. Rules

- **Two ways to know the distance:** `ODOMETER` (start and end readings; distance = end − start, end above start) or `DISTANCE` (entered; shown as "Distance entered"). The distance is always computed or checked on the server (`tripDistance` in `@automate/shared`); a trip without one isn't saved.
- **Dates and times:** a start date, an optional end date (overnight and multi-day trips) and optional start and end times ("HH:MM", India time). The duration exists only when both times are known (`tripDurationMinutes`, across days); a missing value is "—", never 0.
- **The one odometer history** (`OdometerHistoryService`, vehicles module): expenses' readings (fill-ups through their expenses), service records and trips (the start on its first day, the end on its last). A trip's start can't be below a reading from an earlier day, its end can't be above one from a later day (409 on the field), and two trips of a vehicle can't cover the same km. Historical trips are fine whatever the vehicle shows now. Maintenance and Expenses check against the same history.
- **The vehicle's odometer:** a higher end reading raises it; lowering or deleting a trip's reading corrects it down to the highest reading left anywhere in the history (`VehiclesService.correctOdometer`). Never rolled back past a newer reading, and left as it is when no reading is left at all (never 0).
- **Unusual distance:** more than 1,500 km a day (`TRIP_DAY_KM_NOTICE`) shows a warning on the form; it's never refused.
- **Trip types:** 7 system types (`SYSTEM_TRIP_CATEGORIES`, inserted by the migration) and each user's own (unique per user ignoring case, not a system name). The user's own can be renamed and archived (out of the picker; trips keep theirs); the system's can't. A trip's type is never deleted.
- **Vehicle:** a trip stays with its vehicle (delete and log it again to move it); archived vehicles' trips are read-only.

- **Costs** (phase 3): toll, parking, food, accommodation, other, each with amount (Decimal), date, place, payment and notes. **Toll, parking and other are also the vehicle's expenses**: one linked expense per cost (`TRIP_COST_EXPENSE`: Daily usage · Toll / Parking, Other · Miscellaneous; source `TRIP`, sourceId = the cost), written in the cost's transaction under the vehicle's lock, read-only in Expenses ("Edit in Trips" → `/trips/costs/:costId` opens the trip). **Food and accommodation stay with the trip** (not costs of owning the vehicle). Changing a cost's kind creates or removes its expense; deleting a cost or its trip deletes its expense.
- **Actual fuel = linked fill-ups:** a fill-up of the same vehicle can be linked to one trip (`fuel_entries.trip_id`); its total counts for that trip. Fuel still owns it (unlinking or deleting the trip leaves it in Fuel), and no second fuel expense exists. Candidates: the vehicle's fill-ups from the day before the trip to the day after.
- **Actual vs estimated:** actual = recorded costs + linked fill-ups (null when nothing is recorded, never 0); cost per km only when both it and the distance are known. The **fuel estimate** (distance ÷ the Fuel module's average mileage × its average price, in the vehicle's unit) is shown apart and never counted. Overview and Costs-tab per-km figures use only the trips that have a cost.
- **Shortcut:** linked fill-ups count at their full amount (refunds on a fill-up's expense aren't taken off), and trip costs at the amount entered. Fix if refunds on fuel or trip costs become common.

- **Maps and routes** (phase 4, free OpenStreetMap stack): place search through the configured Nominatim-compatible geocoder (`POST /geocode/search`, explicit searches only: Nominatim's policy forbids search-as-you-type), driving routes through an OSRM-compatible server (`ROUTER_URL`; the public demo for development only), map tiles from `VITE_MAP_TILE_URL` (OpenStreetMap's by default, attribution on the map; Leaflet, lazy-loaded). All provider calls are server-side; places and points are personal data, sent in bodies and never logged. No live traffic, no GPS tracking.
- **Places:** origin, destination and up to 10 stops (in order, with optional arrival and departure times) are typed freely or picked from the search, which pins coordinates (pairs only, checked by the database). Unpinned places are just names.
- **Route trips** (`method ROUTE`): origin, destination and every stop must be pinned; **the server fetches the route itself when saving** (outside the transaction; an edit that doesn't touch the places keeps the stored route, a concurrent edit is refused) and the distance is the chosen route's (`routeIndex`; alternatives only when the provider offers them, i.e. without stops). Provider down → 503 and nothing is saved; the form offers Distance or Odometer instead. The stored route (provider, encoded line, duration) is drawn on the trip's map; places without a route show as markers only, and nothing else is ever drawn.

- **Planner** (phase 5): a plan is a row of the same `trips` table with `status` `DRAFT` or `PLANNED` (or `CANCELLED`); done trips are `COMPLETED` and **only completed trips count anywhere** (history, overview, costs, the odometer history, the dashboard). A plan has places, dates (any, usually ahead), a planned distance (entered, or the provider's route for `ROUTE`, fetched by the server as for trips) and the budget's assumptions: mileage, fuel price, toll, parking, stay, other. It never has readings or an actual distance (database check).
- **Budget** (`planBudget`, shared, exact paise maths): fuel = planned distance ÷ mileage × price; total = fuel + toll + parking + stay + other; per km = total ÷ distance. A part that can't be known is null, never guessed (no price → no fuel cost; the rest still adds up). Estimates are never spending and never reach Expenses. New plans start from the vehicle's Fuel figures (average mileage and price), editable.
- **Mark as done:** the same row becomes a completed trip (same id) from what actually happened: odometer readings (checked against the one odometer history, raising the odometer), an entered distance, or the planned route as driven (stored route reused). The plan's places, type, title, stops and notes carry over; its dates and times don't (they're re-entered). The plan's distance, duration and budget stay on the trip: trip details shows **planned vs actual** (distance, duration, budget vs recorded costs + linked fuel). Costs are added on the done trip.
- **Cancel / restore / delete:** cancelled plans are kept under "Cancelled" and can be restored; delete removes a plan. A completed trip is no longer a plan (its API is `/trips`).

- **Road trips** (phase 6): a trip or plan over several days (`endDate` after `date`) gets a **day-by-day itinerary**. Each stop has a `day` (1 = the start date; empty = the previous stop's day) and an `overnight` flag; arrival and departure give the stay. Rules (`checkItinerary`, shared, run with every trip and plan check): days follow the route's order and never go back; no day past the trip's last date (shortening the dates is refused on the end date until stops move); a night is spent at the last stop of its day, never on the last day (it ends at the destination). Each day starts where the previous night was (unknown when no night is set, never guessed). Setting days, stays and nights (`PATCH` with `stops`, places unchanged) keeps the stored route: the provider is called only when the places or the chosen option change (`keepsRoute`). Plans show "Day N of M" while they're on (`tripProgress`); done road trips show days, nights, km a day and actual cost a day (`roadTripSummary`, exact paise).

- **Analytics** (phase 7, `GET /trips/analytics`, done trips only): the period against the one before it (same length, `period.previous`). A change is a percentage only when the previous figure is above 0 (else none is shown, never "+100%"); cost change only when both periods have costs. Distance (average a trip, km a day over the period's days, all time from the first trip; shortest, longest; length bands under 10 / 10–50 / 50–100 / 100–300 / 300+ km), time (average and longest drive over trips with both times; trips per weekday Monday first; time of day from the start time: morning 05–12, afternoon 12–17, evening 17–21, night 21–05, trips without a start time counted apart), actual costs (total, a trip and a km over the trips that have costs), the **fuel estimate** (each vehicle's distance at Fuel's average mileage × price; none when any vehicle lacks those figures, so it's never partial; never counted), vehicles side by side (all vehicles), destinations typed the same way at least twice. Exact tenths-of-km and paise maths (`trip-analytics.ts`).
- **Insights** (deterministic, from the period's records; none under 3 trips): distance change; trips of 100 km or more and their share; a type's share of the distance (40%+, 2+ trips, more than one type); cost per km over the trips with costs (fuel estimate excluded); weekend (more than half) or weekday (80%+) driving, from 5 trips; a 3-month rise or fall in monthly km (the last 3 complete months, all with trips). Each says what it rests on and, where it helps, links to History with the matching filters. Analytics (M8) shows headline trip figures only and links here.

- **Import and export** (phase 9): **CSV export** of the filtered history, every page, in the columns the import reads back (dates ISO, cells that a spreadsheet would run as formulas made inert). **CSV import** into one vehicle from any CSV: its columns are matched to trip fields (`guessTripColumns` from common header names, changeable); each row needs a date and either a distance or both odometer readings, an empty type imports as Other (an unknown one is an error), dates DD/MM/YYYY or ISO. Rows are checked like trips logged by hand; rows with the same date and distance as a recorded trip (or an earlier row) are duplicates, imported only when asked. The **preview runs the real save in a transaction that is rolled back**, so odometer readings are checked against the history and each other before anything is saved; the import runs it again in one transaction, oldest first, and lists rows refused then. Up to 500 rows (80 KB). The **printable report** (`/trips/report`) is the Analytics figures for a period (the fuel estimate apart), trip lengths, vehicles or frequent destinations and every trip, saved as PDF from the browser (no dependency).
- **Backup (M12):** `TripBackupService.backupRows(userId, vehicleId)` returns a vehicle's trips and plans as versioned JSON rows (`TRIP_BACKUP_VERSION`) with stops, costs and linked fill-up ids; `restoreRows(tx, userId, vehicleId, rows, { fuelEntryIds })` recreates them with new ids in the caller's transaction: rows checked like new trips or plans (not against the odometer history: the backup's own readings come back with it), costs with their linked expenses, fill-ups re-linked through the map, types by system key or the user's own name (created when missing), invalid or newer-version rows skipped with a reason. It returns each input row's new trip id and its costs' new ids (`ids`, `costIds`, null when skipped). Rows list their confirmed photos and receipts as `files` (`path` `files/<attachmentId>/<name>`, `costIndex` into `costs`; absent in older backups); Backup carries the bytes and uploads them under the restored ids.

- **Attachments** (phase 10): up to 20 photos and receipts (`TRIP_ATTACHMENTS_MAX`) on a trip or plan, each optionally a cost's receipt; PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC up to 10 MB. Through the one `StorageProvider`, as Documents: the upload is announced (a server-made key), PUT by the browser straight to storage, then confirmed (it arrived, the size matches and its first bytes say it's that type; otherwise it's dropped); only confirmed files are listed; links last 5 minutes and are issued after the ownership check. Deleting a file, its cost or its trip removes the row, and the database's delete trigger queues the object for the storage sweep (which also clears uploads never confirmed after 24 h). An archived vehicle's trips are read-only. Files stay out of the M12 JSON backup (rows only).

Every list is paginated; everything is the signed-in user's.

## 3. Phases

1 · Foundation, recording and overview (with the shared odometer history and dashboard wiring) → 2 · History → 3 · Costs and the Expenses and Fuel links → 4 · Maps and routes (free OpenStreetMap stack) → 5 · Planner → 6 · Road trips → 7 · Analytics and insights → 8 · Remaining integrations → 9 · Import, export and reports → 10 · Attachments and polish. Each phase is approved before the next.

**Built:** phase 1 (trips and trip types, the odometer and entered-distance methods, the shared odometer history used by Maintenance and Expenses too, the overview API and page, log / edit / duplicate / delete, trip details, the trip types dialog, the dashboard's Driving card trips summary, trips in recent activity and the "Log trip" quick action; Stitch "Trips Overview", "Log Trip Form", "Trip Details", "Trip Types Dialog", "Dashboard — Driving & Trips Card") and phase 2 (the History tab: timeline by month or list, search, vehicle, type and popover filters in the URL, sort, the filtered count and distance, bulk change of type and bulk delete; Stitch "Trip History") and phase 3 (trip costs with linked expenses for toll, parking and other, linked fill-ups as actual fuel, actual vs estimated spending, cost per km, the Costs tab, trip costs in lists and the overview, "Edit in Trips" from Expenses) and phase 4 (OpenStreetMap place search, the Route method measured on the provider's route with alternatives, up to 10 ordered stops with times, the route map and markers on trip details, "Open in OpenStreetMap") and phase 5 (the Planner tab with upcoming and cancelled plans, Plan a trip with a live budget from the vehicle's fuel figures, plan details with the route and budget, cancel / restore / delete, Mark as done into a trip, planned vs actual on trip details; Stitch "Trips — Planner", "Plan Trip", "Planned Trip Details", "Mark Trip Done Dialog", "Trip Details — Costs and Plan") and phase 6 (road trips: stop days and nights, the itinerary editor for trips and plans, the day-by-day journey on details, "Day N of M" on plans, the road trip summary;
